## The scholarship route

I knew by second year that I wanted research. I started reading papers from ITC because so many of them worked on the Indus basin. My final year project on flood extent mapping with Sentinel-1 was deliberately modelled on one of their methods.

I applied for the Orange Knowledge Programme and the Erasmus Mundus GEM programme in the same year. The second one came through. Two years later my supervisor offered a PhD position.

## What helped

- A final year project with a clean, reproducible method and a public GitHub repository.
- A recommendation letter from a supervisor who actually knew my work.
- Starting the application process twelve months before the deadline, not three.
