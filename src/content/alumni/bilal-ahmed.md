## Building a freelance survey practice

I started with a borrowed drone and a free copy of WebODM in final year, surveying a housing scheme for a relative's construction firm. The first job paid almost nothing, but the orthomosaic went into my portfolio. Three years later I run two drones and subcontract for larger engineering firms.

## What juniors underestimate

Ground control, accuracy reports, and client communication. The flying is the easy part.
