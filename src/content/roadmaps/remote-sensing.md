---
title: Remote Sensing and Earth Observation
summary: Satellite and aerial imagery analysis for agriculture, forestry, water, disasters and climate. The track with the strongest research and scholarship pipeline.
order: 2
demand: High
tools: [Google Earth Engine, SNAP, QGIS, Python, R]
stages:
  - name: Foundations
    duration: Semester 1 to 3
    skills:
      - Electromagnetic spectrum, sensors, resolution types
      - "Landsat, Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2, MODIS: what each is for"
      - Image preprocessing, band maths, NDVI and friends
      - Supervised and unsupervised classification in QGIS or SNAP
    resources:
      - label: NASA ARSET free trainings
        url: https://appliedsciences.nasa.gov/get-involved/training
      - label: ESA SNAP tutorials
        url: https://step.esa.int/main/doc/tutorials/
  - name: Cloud and scripting
    duration: Semester 3 to 5
    skills:
      - Google Earth Engine JavaScript and Python APIs
      - Time series analysis, phenology, change detection
      - Radar basics with Sentinel-1 for floods and crops
      - Accuracy assessment and reporting
    resources:
      - label: Earth Engine guides
        url: https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/guides
      - label: Spatial Thoughts, End-to-End GEE course
        url: https://courses.spatialthoughts.com/end-to-end-gee.html
  - name: Specialise
    duration: Semester 5 to 7
    skills:
      - "Pick one domain: crop mapping, flood mapping, urban growth, glaciers, air quality"
      - Read ten papers from that domain, reproduce one
      - Machine learning for classification with scikit-learn
      - Scientific writing and figures
    resources:
      - label: Google Earth Engine for Pakistan examples, ICIMOD SERVIR
        url: https://servir.icimod.org/
  - name: Job ready
    duration: Final year
    skills:
      - A reproducible final year project in a chosen domain
      - Applications to SUPARCO, PMD, ICIMOD, WWF, FAO, UNDP, and research groups abroad
      - "Scholarship applications: Erasmus Mundus, DAAD, CSC, Fulbright"
---

Remote sensing is where the institute's graduates most often go abroad for research. Start reading papers early and build a visible, reproducible project.
