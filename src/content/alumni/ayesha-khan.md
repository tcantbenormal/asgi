## How I got here

I joined the institute without a clear plan. In third year a guest lecture on crop acreage estimation from satellite imagery made the whole degree click. I spent the summer teaching myself Google Earth Engine from the official tutorials and used it for my final year project on wheat mapping in Punjab.

That project became my interview talking point. SUPARCO's remote sensing wing asked for someone who could script their classification workflow, and I could show one that already worked.

## What the work looks like

Most weeks are a mix of image preprocessing, writing classification scripts, and preparing reports for provincial agriculture departments. Field validation trips happen twice a season.

## What I wish I had known

- Practise presenting your maps. Half the job is explaining a result to someone who has never opened GIS software.
- Government hiring is slow. Apply early, keep a freelance project running while you wait.
