Every fresh graduate I have interviewed in the last three years had the same CV: the degree, a list of software, and a final year project title. The ones who got hired were the ones who could talk about the project for twenty minutes without notes.

## What the interview is really testing

Nobody expects you to know the organisation's workflow. They want to know whether you can take a vague question, turn it into a spatial analysis, and explain the result. The final year project is the only evidence you have of that, so make it count.

## Three things to prepare

1. **A one-page method summary.** Data sources, preprocessing, the analysis, validation. Bring it printed.
2. **One honest limitation.** If you say your classification was perfect, the panel stops believing you.
3. **A public repository or a shared folder** with the scripts and the final maps. Send the link in your application email.

## What did not matter

Grades, beyond a threshold. Certificates from short online courses. The university's name. The project, and how you explained it, decided it.
