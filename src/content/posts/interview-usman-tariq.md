> Sample interview. Replace with a real conversation.

**You said in your profile that you preferred code to layouts. When did that start?**

Second year, in the first programming course. Everyone else was waiting for it to end. I was building a script to rename a thousand shapefiles. That told me something.

**What was the first project that mattered?**

A Leaflet web map for a Rawalpindi NGO showing their vaccination coverage by union council. It was ugly. But it was live on the internet with my name on it, and I could point at it in every interview after that.

**How did you choose between a job in Pakistan and a master's abroad?**

I did the job first. Eighteen months of real work made the master's application much stronger, and it meant I had savings. I would tell juniors to do the same unless a full scholarship is on the table.

**What does the society portal need from alumni?**

Honesty. Juniors do not need success stories, they need the actual sequence of steps and the mistakes along the way.
