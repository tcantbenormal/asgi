---
title: GIS Analyst
summary: The core track. Spatial data handling, analysis, cartography and reporting for government, utilities, planning and NGOs.
order: 1
demand: High
tools: [QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, PostGIS, Python, Excel]
stages:
  - name: Foundations
    duration: Semester 1 to 2
    skills:
      - Coordinate systems and projections, and why Pakistan uses UTM zones 41 to 43
      - Vector and raster data models, attribute tables, joins
      - "QGIS end to end: load, style, query, lay out, export"
      - "Basic cartographic design: hierarchy, colour, labels, scale"
    resources:
      - label: QGIS training manual
        url: https://docs.qgis.org/latest/en/docs/training_manual/
      - label: Esri MOOC, GIS basics
        url: https://www.esri.com/training/catalog/search/
  - name: Analysis
    duration: Semester 3 to 5
    skills:
      - Overlay, buffer, proximity, network and terrain analysis
      - Geoprocessing models and batch workflows
      - "Spatial databases: PostGIS, SQL for spatial queries"
      - Data quality, topology, metadata
    resources:
      - label: PostGIS workshop
        url: https://postgis.net/workshops/postgis-intro/
  - name: Automation
    duration: Semester 5 to 7
    skills:
      - Python with GeoPandas, Rasterio and PyQGIS
      - Reproducible workflows, version control with Git
      - Reporting with charts and clean map series
    resources:
      - label: Geographic Data Science with Python
        url: https://geographicdata.science/book/intro.html
      - label: Automating GIS Processes, University of Helsinki
        url: https://autogis-site.readthedocs.io/
  - name: Job ready
    duration: Final year
    skills:
      - A portfolio of three complete analyses with write-ups
      - A final year project with a public repository
      - Practice explaining results to non-GIS audiences
      - Apply to NESPAK, provincial planning departments, WASA, NDMA, NGOs, and GIS consultancies
---

Most GIS graduates in Pakistan start here. The track is broad on purpose: an analyst who can also script and manage a database is hired faster than one who only knows one desktop application.
