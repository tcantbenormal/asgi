---
title: Surveying, Drones and Photogrammetry
summary: Field data capture with GNSS, total stations and UAVs, and processing into DEMs, orthomosaics and CAD deliverables for construction and land administration.
order: 4
demand: Growing
tools: [GNSS, Total Station, WebODM, Pix4D, AutoCAD Civil 3D, QGIS]
stages:
  - name: Field fundamentals
    duration: Semester 1 to 3
    skills:
      - Levelling, traversing, total station operation
      - GNSS, RTK and PPK, datum and projection issues in Pakistan
      - Field notes, safety, and working with labour crews
  - name: Drone pipeline
    duration: Semester 3 to 5
    skills:
      - Flight planning, overlap, ground sampling distance
      - Ground control points and accuracy reporting
      - Processing with WebODM, Pix4D or Agisoft Metashape
      - DEM, DSM, orthomosaic and contour generation
    resources:
      - label: OpenDroneMap docs
        url: https://docs.opendronemap.org/
  - name: Deliverables
    duration: Semester 5 to 7
    skills:
      - CAD drafting and exporting to DWG
      - Volume calculation, cut and fill, cross sections
      - Cadastral and land record workflows
      - Civil aviation rules for drone operation in Pakistan
  - name: Job ready
    duration: Final year
    skills:
      - A complete survey report from one real site, with accuracy assessment
      - Apply to NESPAK, NHA contractors, housing developers, mining firms, and survey consultancies, or start freelance
---

The surveying market in Pakistan is large and undersupplied with graduates who can run the full pipeline from field to deliverable. It is also the easiest track to start freelancing in.
